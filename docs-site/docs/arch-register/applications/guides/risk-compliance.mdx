# Risk & Compliance

Risk & Compliance brings together risk review, control coverage, retention assignments, and assessment status. The
application reports on shared workspace entities and typed relations; it does not replace the catalog records or the
project assessment workflow.

## Enable and configure the application

In **Workspace Settings → Applications & Capabilities**, configure **Risk & Compliance** and bind a **Risk** entity
schema. **Control**, **Framework**, **Compliance Requirement**, and **Data Entity** bindings are optional. The Data
Entity binding scopes the asset coverage views; Control and related bindings add control and framework coverage where
configured. The seeded default workspace has Risk & Compliance configured.

Application access is configured separately in the capability's **Access** tab. Members also need workspace access and
a valid capability binding. See [Workspace Templates and Capabilities](../../admin/workspace-templates-capabilities) and
[Roles and Permissions](../../admin/roles-permissions).

**Retention** uses the separate workspace-wide **Retention** capability. Configure its Policy entity schema and
**Subject to Retention Policy** relation schema under **Applications & Capabilities** to enable that section; it can be
configured independently of the Risk & Compliance capability. The capability also maps **Period** to a duration field and **Activated from** to a date field (workspaces created before
the duration field type can keep mapping **Duration** to a number field and **Time unit** to a select field); the default catalog uses fields with those
standard roles, while a custom model can map its own fields.

## Review risks, controls, and assessments

**Overview** is a configurable dashboard summarizing risks outside appetite, control coverage, a risk matrix, and upcoming
risk or control assessments. Use **Risks** to filter by category, status and owner in the sidebar, then switch between the **Register** tab and a
5×5 likelihood and impact matrix on the **Matrix** tab. The matrix can show inherent or residual risk. Open a risk to review its scores, mitigating controls, and entities it affects.

In **Controls**, filter by type and operating effectiveness in the sidebar and use the **Library** tab to inspect
controls. Switch to **Coverage** to review coverage by risk or information asset, or to **Controls × Risks** (cells are coloured by the control's
operating effectiveness) or **Controls × Data Entities** to trace controls. Coverage of
information assets depends on a Data Entity binding and the corresponding Control Protection relation in the workspace
model.

**Retention** lists policy assignments to governed entity categories, including the assigned period and activation
date. It is a configurable dashboard; use the sidebar to narrow the register to one retention policy. **Assessments** is a configurable dashboard of project assessments that target the Risk
schema and, when configured, the Control schema: lists of reviews and tests due, plus progress tables per schema. Open a
row to continue in its owning project's Assessments tab; create, edit, and answer assessments there.

## Retention scope and shared workflows

Retention assignments apply a policy to a Data Entity category, not to each individual record. The section therefore
lists what is assigned and since when; it does not calculate per-record disposal dates or an overdue disposal queue.

Risk, Control, Framework, Requirement, and Data Entity records are maintained in [Entities](../../use/entities), using
the schemas and relations described in [Data Modeling and Schemas](../../use/data-modeling-schemas). Use [Entity Views](../../use/entity-views)
and [Entity Relationship Views](../../use/entity-relationship-views) to inspect the wider catalog. For assessment setup
and responses, see [Projects](../../use/projects) and [Assessments](../../use/assessments); for approval workflows, see
[Governance and Approvals](../../use/governance). Administrators configure bindings in [Workspace Settings](../../admin/workspace-settings)
and access in [Roles and Permissions](../../admin/roles-permissions).
