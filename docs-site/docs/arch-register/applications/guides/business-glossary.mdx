# Business Glossary

The Business Glossary application helps members find and review governed business terms. It uses the workspace's
entity schemas for term and category data, while providing glossary-specific search, quality filters, and usage
information.

## Open the glossary

Select **Business Glossary** from the application switcher. The app is available only when the workspace has a valid
glossary capability configuration and the member has application access. If the app is unavailable, ask a workspace
administrator to check [Applications & Capabilities](../../admin/workspace-settings) and the
[application access policy](../../admin/roles-permissions#application-access).

## Find terms

Search by a term's canonical name, synonym, or abbreviation. Use the category, owner, lifecycle, and quality filters to
narrow the results. Quality filters identify unused, conflicting, deprecated, and ownerless terms. Select a term to
open its entity details and review its configured fields, metadata, and related usage.

## Create and maintain terms

Term definitions, aliases, categories, ownership, lifecycle, and status are maintained as entity data. Create or edit
terms from **Entities** using the workspace's glossary term schema; manage category records through the configured
category schema. Existing entity permissions, history, and approval workflows apply. See [Entities](../../use/entities),
[Data Modeling and Schemas](../../use/data-modeling-schemas), and [Governance and Approvals](../../use/governance).

## Review term usage

The glossary reports usage across entities, typed relations, Markdown, projects, and diagrams. Usage is permission-aware,
so the report only exposes material the current member can access. Use these reports alongside the term quality filters
to identify records that may need an owner, a definition, or a status review.
