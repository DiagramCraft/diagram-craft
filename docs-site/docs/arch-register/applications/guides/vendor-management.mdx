# Vendor Management

Vendor Management summarizes vendor records, their contracts, renewal dates, spend, and risk. It reads the workspace
catalog: vendors and contracts are entity records, and contract links to vendors and systems provide the relationships
used by the application.

## Enable and configure the application

In **Workspace Settings → Applications & Capabilities**, configure **Vendor Management** and bind a **Vendor** entity
schema. The Vendor binding is required. **Contract** and **Technology Release** schemas are optional; without a Contract
binding, contract, renewal, and spend data are unavailable. Technology Release is only needed for the technology
end-of-life exposure view in **Risk**. The default catalog binds its Vendor, Contract, and Technology Release schemas
automatically.

Set who can open the application in its **Access** tab. Members need workspace access, a valid capability binding, and
application access. See [Workspace Settings](../../admin/workspace-settings), [Workspace Templates and Capabilities](../../admin/workspace-templates-capabilities),
and [Roles and Permissions](../../admin/roles-permissions).

## Review vendors, contracts, and spend

**Overview** is a configurable dashboard summarizing contracted spend, upcoming renewals, auto-renewing contracts,
vendors above the configured risk bands, and technology releases nearing end of life. Select a row to open the related
vendor, contract, or release in its drawer. Edit the dashboard to add, remove, or reconfigure its widgets.

Use **Vendors** to browse the vendor register with its spend, risk, and next renewal, and filter it by tier, category,
or relationship owner. A vendor drawer combines its
catalog attributes and risk profile with spend from its contracts, those contracts, and the systems they serve.

In **Contracts**, filter contracts by type or vendor. Switch between the register and the
12-month renewal calendar to review dates and amounts, or use **Timelines** to compare contract terms across time.
Overdue contracts appear in the current month; contracts beyond the calendar window or without an end date remain
available in the register. The timeline draws a bar only when both start and end dates are available; an auto-renewing
contract with a notice period also shows its notice date. Contracts missing either date remain in the register and are
counted below the timeline.

Use **Spend** to group annualized spend by vendor or cost centre. The capability grouping option currently has no data
because contracts have no Contract-to-capability link. The **Risk** section combines a criticality and risk-band matrix,
a vendor risk register, and an optional technology end-of-life exposure table. The exposure table requires a Technology
Release binding and catalog links from Systems to Technology Release records; it is hidden when those are missing.

## Current limitations and shared workflows

Create and maintain vendor and contract records in [Entities](../../use/entities); the application provides focused
registers and drawers. See [Data Modeling and Schemas](../../use/data-modeling-schemas)
and [Entity Relationship Views](../../use/entity-relationship-views) for the shared catalog model. [Projects](../../use/projects),
[Assessments](../../use/assessments), and [Governance and Approvals](../../use/governance) describe shared planning and
review workflows. Administrators configure schemas and application access in [Workspace Settings](../../admin/workspace-settings)
and [Roles and Permissions](../../admin/roles-permissions).
