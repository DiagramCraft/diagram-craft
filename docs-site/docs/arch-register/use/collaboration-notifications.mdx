# Collaboration And Notifications

Arch Register keeps collaboration close to the records. Watch entities for changes, discuss records and content where
they live, and use the notification feed to return to activity that needs your attention.

## Watch Entities

Select the watch control on an entity detail screen to follow that record. Watched entities generate notifications
when relevant changes occur. The bell menu's **Watching** tab lists the entities you follow; open a record from the
list or unwatch one you no longer need to follow.

## Discuss Records And Content

Entity detail screens have a **Discussions** tab for threads attached to the record. Supported wiki pages and
documents also support inline comments and discussion threads, so reviewers can keep feedback beside the content it
refers to. The top-bar discussions menu collects threads you participate in.

Comment activity can notify the person who owns an entity or content item when someone comments on it, and can notify
someone when another person replies to their comment. Arch Register excludes the person who posted the comment, and
notifications respect the recipient's current access to the related content.

## Use The Notification Feed

The bell in the top bar opens the consolidated in-app feed. It includes:

- changes on entities you watch
- comment activity on entities and content you own, or replies to your comments
- governance action items assigned to you

Open a notification to visit its related entity, content, or [My Work](governance). You can clear an individual item
or mark all notifications read. An unread count appears on the bell. Governance action-item notifications clear
automatically when the task is resolved, superseded, or cancelled; other feed items remain in the feed until cleared.

## Set Notification Preferences

Open **Account settings → Notifications** to choose delivery by notification type and channel. In-app delivery is on
by default for normal notification types and off by default for reminder types. Email delivery is available when the
deployment has configured it; Slack and SMS are not currently deliverable.

Preferences apply per user and workspace and only affect future notifications. Changing a preference does not remove
existing feed items.
